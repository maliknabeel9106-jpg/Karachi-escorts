Privacy Policy

Client privacy and confidentiality sit at the core of Karachi Escorts (karachiescorts.org).

1. Zero Client Account or Identity Retention

We do not require account creation, government ID card uploads, or payment card numbers to browse companions or arrange an appointment. Inquiries sent through our reservation forms remain strictly in your browser until you choose to transmit your booking message via WhatsApp.

2. Encrypted Concierge Communications

Direct booking inquiries conducted over WhatsApp (+92 307 4277717) benefit from end-to-end messaging encryption. Appointment logistics—including preferred companion, hotel or neighborhood, and timing—are cleared promptly following appointment completion.

3. Browser Storage & Preferences

This static website uses only minimal browser local storage (`karachi_escorts_theme`) to remember your chosen dark or light appearance setting between page visits. We do not use advertising trackers or sell visitor browsing behavior to third parties.

4. Financial Discretion

Because Karachi Escorts operates exclusively on a zero-advance cash-on-arrival basis, no bank statements, merchant billing descriptors, or card transaction histories are generated by our website.

5. Contact for Privacy Questions

If you have privacy inquiries regarding this website, please email info@karachiescorts.org.
