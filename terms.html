Terms & Conditions

Standards of eligibility, respectful conduct, and cash-on-arrival settlement governing appointments arranged through karachiescorts.org.

1. 18+ Adult Eligibility

All visitors accessing karachiescorts.org and booking companionship services confirm they are at least 18 years of age (or the age of majority in their jurisdiction). Every companion represented on this site is verified to be an adult aged 21 or older.

2. Scope of Companionship

Rates published on this website reflect compensation strictly for time, conversation, dinner dates, and social companionship. Any activities beyond legal, consensual companionship between adults remain strictly personal matters outside agency representation.

3. Zero Advance & Cash-on-Arrival Rule

  • Karachi Escorts never asks for upfront mobile wallet deposits, gift cards, or online wire transfers before an in-person meeting.
  • Clients settle the agreed honorarium directly in cash upon verifying their companion's identity in person.
  • Always verify our official phone/WhatsApp number (+92 307 4277717) before confirming an appointment.

4. Respect, Consent & Cancellations

Mutual respect, hygiene, and personal boundaries are mandatory during all in-call and out-call visits. Photography or video recording without explicit consent is strictly prohibited. Please notify the concierge promptly in the event of schedule changes prior to dispatch.
